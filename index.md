This devroom is dedicated to bringing together the people behind open source data streaming technologies to discuss recent innovations, emerging trends, and the role these projects play in modern event-driven systems.

Our focus is not on promoting any single technology. Instead, we aim to encourage knowledge sharing across communities, create new connections between projects, and foster collaboration on the technical and organizational challenges that lie ahead.

## Where

[FOSDEM 2027](https://fosdem.org/2027/) will be hosted in person at [ULB Solbosch Campus, Brussels, Belgium](https://fosdem.org/2027/practical/transportation/).

## Suggested Topics

* Stream processing architectures and design patterns (e.g., event sourcing, CQRS, streaming microservices)
* Real-time analytics and complex event processing
* Streaming SQL engines and stream-table duality
* Change data capture (CDC) and real-time data integration
* Exactly-once semantics and fault-tolerance in streaming systems
* Scaling and operating streaming platforms in production
* Stream processing on Kubernetes and cloud-native deployments
* AI/ML with streaming data (real-time inference, online learning, feature stores)
* Streaming data governance, lineage, and observability
* IoT and edge computing with data streams
* Stories from end-users (both successes and failures)
* Emerging trends and novel use cases in data streaming

These are just suggestions and we encourage you to be creative with your proposals!

A few notes:

*   You must be available in person to present your talk at FOSDEM.
*   Speaking slots are 25 minutes in length (including Q&A) with 5 minutes between sessions. Note that due to time constraints, number of submissions, etc., we may end up offering a slot duration different than 25 minutes.
*   FOSDEM is a [FLOSS](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Free_and_open-source_software) community event, so please ensure your topic is appropriate (i.e., no sales pitches).
